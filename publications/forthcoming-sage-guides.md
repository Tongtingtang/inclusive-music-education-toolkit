# Forthcoming SAGE Research Methods Contributions

## Inclusive Research Methodologies (Accepted for Publication)

### Overview

This document summarizes a series of accepted contributions to the SAGE Research Methods collection on Inclusive Research Methodologies.

The guides explore how embodied musical practice, spatial reasoning, and participatory approaches may support more inclusive forms of research design and knowledge production.

Together, the three contributions investigate how musical activities can function not merely as artistic practices, but also as methodological tools for participation, communication, and inquiry.

# Guide 1

## Body-based Music Practice as a Method for Enabling Research Participation in Economically Marginalized Communities

### Focus

This guide explores how body-based musical activities may support participation in research contexts where conventional approaches rely heavily on literacy, technical expertise, or access to specialized resources.

The guide investigates how movement, rhythm, collective participation, and embodied interaction may create alternative pathways for engagement.

### Key Themes

* embodied participation
* community engagement
* inclusive research design
* alternative forms of communication
* participatory methodologies

### Relationship to This Repository

This guide provides methodological foundations for several principles discussed within:

* Embodied Music Pedagogy
* Kalisarmonia
* Deaf-led Innovation

# Guide 2

## Directional Chord Symbols and Sumerian Counting as Embodied Tools for Inclusive Research Participation within STEM Music Integration

### Focus

This guide investigates how directional representations and embodied numerical structures may support participation in interdisciplinary learning and research environments.

The guide explores how symbolic systems can become more accessible when relationships are represented through direction, movement, and spatial orientation.

### Key Themes

* directional representation
* embodied cognition
* STEM integration
* spatial reasoning
* inclusive participation

### Relationship to This Repository

This guide is closely related to:

* Directional Chord System (DCS)
* Jacob's Hand
* Kalisarmonia

# Guide 3

## Embodied Musical Practice as a Rigorous Framework for Inclusive and Participatory Research Design

### Focus

This guide argues that embodied musical practice can function as a rigorous methodological framework rather than merely a creative activity.

The guide explores how musical participation may contribute to data generation, collaborative inquiry, community engagement, and inclusive forms of knowledge production.

### Key Themes

* artistic research
* participatory inquiry
* embodied methodology
* collaborative knowledge production
* inclusive research design

### Relationship to This Repository

This guide provides a broader methodological context for the entire Inclusive Music Education Toolkit.

# Shared Themes Across the Three Guides

Although each guide addresses a different topic, several themes connect them.

These include:

* embodied knowledge
* participation
* accessibility
* inclusion
* spatial understanding
* cultural responsiveness
* community engagement

Together, the guides contribute to a growing conversation about how research methods can become more inclusive without sacrificing rigor.
