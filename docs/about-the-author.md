# About the Author

## Soegiarto Hartono

Soegiarto Hartono is an Indonesian Deaf researcher, educator, speaker, and cultural practitioner whose work explores the intersection of music, embodiment, inclusion, and cultural knowledge systems.

His research and educational activities focus on developing approaches that make musical knowledge more accessible through spatial, visual, tactile, and embodied learning methods. Drawing from both traditional musical practices and contemporary interdisciplinary research, he investigates how music can be understood not only through sound, but also through movement, relationships, balance, and physical experience.

## Research and Educational Contributions

Soegiarto Hartono is the developer of several educational and research frameworks that explore alternative pathways to musical understanding:

### Jacob's Hand

An ongoing embodied research framework that investigates how musical relationships may be represented through hand-based spatial structures.

### Kolintang Hand

A pedagogical system developed through practical experience in teaching and performing Minahasan Kolintang music, emphasizing visual communication, ensemble coordination, and accessibility.

### Directional Chord System

A visual-spatial approach to harmony that explores how directional movement and spatial relationships can support musical understanding.

## Vision

The long-term goal of his work is to contribute to a broader understanding of music as an embodied and relational form of knowledge.

Through research, teaching, cultural engagement, and open educational resources, he seeks to support approaches that connect artistic practice, accessibility, cultural heritage, and interdisciplinary inquiry.
